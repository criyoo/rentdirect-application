# About RentDirect

## Renting Shouldn't Be This Difficult

Finding a rental property in Nigeria, particularly in cities like Lagos, has become increasingly frustrating for both landlords and tenants.

Tenants are often forced to navigate a complicated system filled with multiple agents, hidden fees, inspection charges, fake listings, unclear processes, and the constant risk of rental scams. Many spend weeks searching for a property, only to discover that the advertised property does not exist, the price has changed, or additional fees have suddenly appeared.

Landlords face a different set of challenges. Finding trustworthy tenants can be difficult. Property owners often have limited visibility into who is applying for their property, rely heavily on third parties to manage the rental process, and have little control over the tenant selection experience. In many cases, landlords also struggle with fraudulent applicants, poor communication, and unnecessary delays in securing quality tenants.

RentDirect was created to solve these problems.

## Our Mission

Our mission is simple:

**To create the most transparent, trusted, and efficient rental marketplace where verified landlords connect directly with verified tenants.**

We believe renting a home should not involve hidden fees, misinformation, unnecessary middlemen, or uncertainty. Both landlords and tenants deserve a platform built on trust, transparency, and accountability.

## Why RentDirect Exists

The traditional rental process often places agents and intermediaries at the center of every transaction. While many agents provide valuable services, the process has become increasingly expensive, fragmented, and difficult to navigate.

RentDirect was built for:

### Landlords who

* Want direct access to prospective tenants.
* Prefer managing their properties themselves.
* Want greater control over the rental process.
* Need access to verified, quality tenants.
* Want to reduce dependency on intermediaries.
* Value transparency and documented communication.
* Want better visibility into tenant applications and screening.

### Tenants who

* Are tired of dealing with multiple agents.
* Want to avoid excessive agency and agreement fees.
* Do not want to pay inspection or viewing fees before renting.
* Want to deal directly with property owners.
* Want protection from scams and fraudulent listings.
* Need access to verified rental properties.
* Want a clear and transparent rental process from start to finish.
