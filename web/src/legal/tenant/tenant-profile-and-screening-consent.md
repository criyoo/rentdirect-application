# RENTDIRECT TENANT PROFILE AND SCREENING CONSENT

## TENANT DATA, GUARANTOR INFORMATION, AND LANDLORD ACCESS

**Effective Date:** ____________________  
**Last Updated:** ____________________

This document explains the Tenant-specific consent for structured profile and screening information. It supplements the Privacy Policy and Verification and Screening Consent. It is not a promise that a Landlord will accept an application.

## 1. INFORMATION YOU MAY PROVIDE

The Tenant profile may include:

* identity, date of birth, gender, nationality, origin, and residence;
* employment or self-employment information;
* income, savings, expenses, current rent, and affordability information;
* current and previous Landlord information and rental history;
* guarantor identity, contact, occupation, employer, and address;
* household, occupants, children, pets, smoking, work-from-home, and home-business details;
* social profile links;
* declarations about convictions, evictions, rent arrears, tenancy litigation, and Landlord disputes; and
* supporting documents such as government ID, passport photograph, utility bill, employment evidence, bank or tax records, and guarantor documents.

Only provide information that is relevant, accurate, lawful to provide, and requested for a legitimate rental or verification purpose.

## 2. YOUR REPRESENTATIONS

By submitting the profile, you represent that:

* you are the person described by the account;
* the information is accurate, current, and complete to the best of your knowledge;
* you have authority or a lawful basis to submit information about a guarantor, employer, household member, or previous Landlord;
* documents have not been forged, altered, or obtained unlawfully; and
* you will correct material changes and respond to reasonable clarification requests.

## 3. USE OF THE PROFILE

RentDirect may use the profile to:

* perform identity and account verification;
* create a Tenant screening summary or category-level indicator;
* support enquiries, applications, Bookings, payment risk, and rental progress;
* show a limited profile to a Landlord connected to a legitimate rental interaction; and
* protect the marketplace, respond to complaints, and comply with law.

## 4. WHAT A LANDLORD MAY SEE

Depending on the Platform feature and the Tenant’s relationship with the Landlord, a Landlord may see a safe summary such as name, photo, email-verification status, identity or Tenant-profile status, residence area, employment category, household information, rental history indicators, declarations, enquiry/application counts, and relevant screening categories.

RentDirect seeks to limit direct exposure of NIN, BVN, full bank details, identity-document images, bank statements, and other high-risk information. Access controls may change as the Platform evolves or as law requires.

## 5. SCREENING IS NOT A DECISION

A screening summary, category, score, verification indicator, or public Tenant profile is only an information tool. It does not prove that you are a suitable Tenant, guarantee affordability, or require a Landlord to accept you. A Landlord remains responsible for a lawful and non-discriminatory rental decision.

RentDirect does not guarantee that a screening result is complete, current, error-free, or predictive of future conduct.

## 6. GUARANTOR AND THIRD-PARTY INFORMATION

Before providing guarantor, employer, household, or previous-Landlord details, you should provide any notice required by law and ensure that the information is accurate. RentDirect may contact or verify a third party only for a stated lawful purpose and may require evidence of authority.

Do not submit another person’s identity number, account credentials, or document unless you are authorised to do so.

## 7. CONSENT TO VERIFICATION

By submitting the profile or selecting a verification consent, you authorise RentDirect and its authorised providers to process the information for the purposes described in the Privacy Policy and Verification Consent. This may include NIN or BVN checks, document review, consistency checks, fraud prevention, and manual or assisted screening.

You may request correction, withdrawal, deletion, or restriction through **info@rentdirect.homes**, subject to lawful retention, open Bookings or payments, fraud prevention, other persons’ rights, and applicable law. Withdrawal may make profile, application, payment, or contact features unavailable.

## 8. PRIVACY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

RentDirect will process the profile under its Privacy Policy. Landlords and other Users must use Tenant information only for the legitimate rental purpose for which access was granted and must not publish, sell, share, or reuse it for unrelated marketing, employment, credit, political, or other purposes.

Report suspected misuse promptly through support or the complaint flow.

## 9. UPDATES AND RETENTION

You should update a profile when material information changes. RentDirect may retain profile and screening records for account security, rental history, dispute handling, fraud prevention, legal claims, and other lawful purposes. Data is deleted, anonymised, or securely disposed of when no longer required, subject to lawful exceptions.

## 10. ACCEPTANCE

By submitting a Tenant profile, document, verification request, application, or Booking, you acknowledge that the profile may be processed and shared in a limited, role-appropriate way for legitimate rental decisions and Platform safety, and that you remain responsible for the accuracy and lawful submission of the information.
