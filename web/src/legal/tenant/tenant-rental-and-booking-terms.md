# RENTDIRECT TENANT RENTAL AND BOOKING TERMS

## TENANT-SPECIFIC TERMS

**Effective Date:** ____________________  
**Last Updated:** ____________________

These Tenant Rental and Booking Terms apply to a User who searches for, enquires about, applies for, books, pays for, or occupies a property through RentDirect. They supplement the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Verification and Screening Consent, Marketplace and Rental Disclaimer, and Subscription and Payment Policy.

## 1. TENANT ELIGIBILITY AND ACCOUNT

You must be at least eighteen (18) years old, use your own account, complete email verification where required, and provide accurate information. You must not create an application, Booking, or payment workflow using another person’s identity or without genuine intention to rent.

You are responsible for keeping your account secure and for promptly reporting a suspected compromise, identity misuse, or payment error.

## 2. TENANT PROFILE AND VERIFICATION

RentDirect may ask you to complete a Tenant profile containing biodata, residence, employment, affordability, rental history, household, guarantor, Landlord, social-presence, declaration, and supporting-document information. Certain features may require NIN, BVN, identity, or other verification.

You must provide information that is accurate, current, complete, and lawfully submitted. You must update material changes and must not conceal information that the Platform or a lawful rental process requires.

Verification and screening can improve the quality of information available to a Landlord, but do not guarantee acceptance, affordability, suitability, safety, or a tenancy.

## 3. SEARCH, FAVOURITES, AND ENQUIRIES

Search results, filters, photos, Listing details, rent, deposit, amenities, availability, and verification indicators are provided to support review. They may contain errors or change. A favourite does not reserve a property.

An enquiry is a request to communicate. It is not an offer accepted by a Landlord, a reservation, a viewing guarantee, or a tenancy application unless the Platform expressly labels it as such.

You must use messages for a legitimate property-related purpose, communicate respectfully, and avoid sending sensitive information or payment credentials through chat.

## 4. INDEPENDENT PROPERTY REVIEW

Before applying or paying, you should inspect the property, confirm the person’s identity and authority to let, review the property area and condition, ask about utilities and charges, verify availability, and obtain independent advice where appropriate.

Do not rely solely on a verification badge, photo, map pin, recommendation, or message. Use safe viewing practices and do not pay a person who asks you to bypass a displayed payment or verification process without understanding the risk.

## 5. APPLICATION AND BOOKING

When you select a property and start a Booking, you must provide accurate dates, household information, and other requested details. A Booking may remain pending until a Landlord accepts it, payment is confirmed, verification conditions are satisfied, or the parties sign a tenancy agreement.

A Booking record does not itself:

* guarantee Landlord acceptance;
* transfer possession or title;
* create a tenancy agreement;
* guarantee that a property remains available; or
* replace a signed agreement required by law.

The parties must agree the property, term, rent, deposit, permitted use, repairs, utilities, service charges, notices, and other tenancy terms in writing.

## 6. RENT, DEPOSIT, AND FEES

The checkout may show annual rent, a refundable security deposit, an administration or service fee, the total, the amount paid, and the remaining balance. The current RentDirect calculation uses:

* refundable security deposit: 10% of annual rent; and
* administration fee: 10% of annual rent.

The final checkout controls. The security deposit remains subject to the signed tenancy agreement, the property’s actual condition, lawful deductions, and applicable law. The administration or service fee is a Platform charge for the services described at checkout and is not automatically a Landlord deposit.

## 7. PAYMENTS

Use only the payment methods and account information presented through the Platform. Supported methods may include card and bank transfer through Flutterwave. A payment is not complete until the provider and RentDirect record it as completed.

You must not:

* submit a payment method you are not authorised to use;
* pay more than the remaining balance;
* upload a fake payment confirmation;
* share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or full card details; or
* arrange an undisclosed payment intended to defeat Platform controls.

Keep receipts and payment references. Report duplicate, failed, reversed, or unrecognised payments promptly.

## 8. CANCELLATION, REFUNDS, AND FAILED TRANSACTIONS

A Tenant may request cancellation or a refund through the available support or payment process. The result depends on payment status, the Booking, the signed tenancy agreement, the reason and timing of cancellation, provider rules, Landlord obligations, and applicable law.

RentDirect does not promise that every cancelled Booking will produce an immediate or full refund. A completed payment, provider reversal, or disputed deposit may require investigation. Mandatory consumer or tenancy rights are not excluded.

## 9. TENANCY PERFORMANCE

If you enter a tenancy, you must:

* pay rent, deposit, utilities, service charges, and other agreed amounts when due;
* use the property only for the permitted purpose and occupancy;
* comply with lawful property rules, including smoking, pet, commercial-activity, and short-let conditions;
* care for the property and promptly report defects, damage, safety issues, or unauthorised access;
* avoid illegal activity, nuisance, violence, harassment, and unreasonable interference with neighbours;
* obtain consent before alterations, subletting, assignment, or additional occupants where required; and
* comply with the signed tenancy agreement and applicable law.

RentDirect does not decide eviction, possession, repairs, or deposit disputes unless a separate written service expressly says it will.

## 10. RENTAL PROGRESS

The Tenant dashboard may contain milestones such as viewing booked, property viewed, agreement signed, deposit paid, rent paid in full, inventory completed, key collected, and payment or settlement notices. You must update only milestones within your knowledge and must not mark a step complete falsely.

Progress records support communication and payment operations but do not prove that a party has performed every legal obligation.

## 11. TENANT DATA AND GUARANTORS

You are responsible for obtaining a lawful basis before submitting another person’s details. Tell guarantors, employers, referees, household members, or previous Landlords that their information may be processed for the stated rental and verification purposes where required.

Landlord access to Tenant data is limited by the Platform feature, role, relationship, and privacy controls. Do not ask a Landlord to use your information for unrelated purposes.

## 12. COMMUNICATIONS AND SAFETY

You should keep important questions, approvals, prices, payment confirmations, and changes recorded in the Platform or in a signed document. RentDirect may moderate messages for safety, policy enforcement, and transaction support.

For an emergency or immediate threat, contact the appropriate emergency service before contacting RentDirect.

## 13. REVIEWS AND FEEDBACK

Only submit an honest review based on a genuine experience. Do not use a review to threaten, extort, discriminate, reveal private information, or publish an allegation you cannot responsibly support. Use the complaint or support process for urgent or confidential matters.

## 14. BREACH AND ACCESS RESTRICTIONS

RentDirect may suspend your account, remove Content, pause a Booking or payment, require re-verification, or terminate access if you submit false information, misuse personal data, engage in unsafe conduct, commit payment fraud, or breach the Platform policies.

## 15. NO GUARANTEE

RentDirect does not guarantee that a search will produce a suitable property, that a Landlord will respond or accept you, that a Listing is safe or available, or that your tenancy will continue without dispute. Make independent decisions and obtain professional advice where necessary.

## 16. CONTACT AND ACCEPTANCE

Tenant support and complaints: **info@rentdirect.homes**. Include your account email, Listing or Booking reference, payment reference, dates, and a clear description. Do not include passwords, PINs, OTPs, or full payment-card details.

By creating a Tenant account, submitting a profile, opening a Booking, or paying through RentDirect, you acknowledge these Tenant Terms and the linked Platform policies.
