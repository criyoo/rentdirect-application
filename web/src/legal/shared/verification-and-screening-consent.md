## 1. PURPOSE

RentDirect uses verification and screening workflows to improve trust in a direct rental marketplace. Depending on the role and feature, the process may help to:

* confirm that an account is linked to a real person or organisation;
* compare submitted identity, corporate, or property information with lawful sources;
* reduce impersonation, forged documents, fake Listings, payment fraud, and abuse;
* show proportionate verification indicators or screening categories; and
* support safer decisions by Tenants, Landlords, and RentDirect operations.

Verification is a risk-reduction tool. It is not a certification, guarantee, insurance policy, recommendation, or endorsement.

## 2. CONSENT AND AUTHORITY

By requesting verification, uploading supporting information, selecting a consent control, submitting a Tenant profile, creating a Listing, or continuing with a feature that requires verification, you authorise RentDirect and its authorised providers to collect, review, compare, store, and use relevant information for the purposes described here and in the Privacy Policy.

You confirm that:

* the information and documents are authentic and lawfully obtained;
* you are entitled to provide them;
* information about a guarantor, employer, Landlord, property manager, company, or other person is provided with an appropriate lawful basis;
* you will cooperate with reasonable clarification requests; and
* you will notify RentDirect if information becomes inaccurate or your authority changes.

Where a separate, express consent is required by applicable law, RentDirect will request it before the relevant check.

## 3. LANDLORD VERIFICATION

### 3.1 Individual Landlords

RentDirect may process name, date of birth, mobile, NIN, BVN, residence, identity documents, profile information, bank and payout information, and other evidence needed to assess identity and authority.

### 3.2 Corporate Landlords

RentDirect may process registered or trading name, CAC registration details, company address, contact information, tax or licence information where relevant, authorised representative identity, proof of authority, and corporate documents.

### 3.3 Property and Physical Verification

For a Listing, RentDirect may review ownership or authority documents, property images, address and coordinates, availability, property information, and physical-property evidence or status. A physical-property status may be unverified, pending, or verified according to the applicable workflow.

## 4. TENANT VERIFICATION AND SCREENING

Tenant verification may include identity information, NIN, BVN, date of birth, profile and residence details, and supporting documents. Tenant screening information may include employment, affordability, rental history, guarantor, household, social presence, and declarations submitted by the Tenant.

Landlords may receive a limited, role-appropriate profile or screening summary where the Tenant has a relevant enquiry, application, Booking, or another permitted relationship. RentDirect seeks to avoid exposing full bank details, identity numbers, document images, and unrelated sensitive information.

## 5. VERIFICATION PROVIDERS AND SOURCES

RentDirect may use manual review, lawful public or official records, and authorised providers such as Dikript, Prembly, payment providers, identity services, corporate registries, document-review tools, and security providers. Provider names, endpoints, data locations, and retention periods may change as the Platform evolves.

Information may be compared for consistency, including name, date of birth, mobile, company name, registration number, address, and other relevant fields. A mismatch may require clarification or cause a check to be marked pending, rejected, or failed.

## 6. POSSIBLE OUTCOMES

The Platform may record or display statuses such as:

* **Unverified:** no completed check or current evidence;
* **Pending:** a submission or check is awaiting processing;
* **Under review:** additional human or provider review is required;
* **Verified/approved:** the relevant evidence met the current workflow requirements;
* **Partially verified:** some, but not all, information was confirmed; or
* **Rejected/failed:** the evidence did not satisfy the current requirements or could not be confirmed.

RentDirect may change a status if information expires, is withdrawn, becomes inaccurate, a provider corrects a result, or new risk information arises.

## 7. SCREENING AND DECISIONS

Screening categories, summaries, scores, or indicators are informational inputs. Landlords and Tenants must make independent, lawful, and non-discriminatory decisions. RentDirect does not guarantee that an approved User is suitable, solvent, safe, honest, or likely to perform a tenancy.

Automated or assisted checks may be used to identify inconsistencies, risk, or missing information. Where applicable law gives you rights in relation to a significant automated decision, contact RentDirect for information and review options.

## 8. DOCUMENT AUTHENTICITY AND SECURITY

Do not upload unnecessary documents. Remove unrelated information where possible. Do not send passwords, full card details, OTPs, or PINs. RentDirect may reject files that are unreadable, excessive, malicious, irrelevant, unlawfully obtained, or unsafe to store.

We apply reasonable access and security controls, but no online storage or transmission is risk-free. You are responsible for the accuracy and lawful submission of your documents.

## 9. WITHDRAWAL, CORRECTION, AND RE-CHECKS

You may request correction, withdrawal, deletion, or restriction of verification information subject to applicable law, the need to protect other people, open transactions, fraud prevention, recordkeeping, or legal claims. Withdrawal may make a feature unavailable or change a status to unverified.

RentDirect may request a fresh check where documents expire, information changes, a transaction requires it, or the reliability of a previous result is reasonably questioned.

## 10. RETENTION AND SHARING

Verification and screening records may be retained for the period reasonably required for account safety, transaction records, fraud prevention, disputes, legal obligations, audit, and service integrity. Sharing is limited by role, feature, relationship, consent, and applicable law as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 11. FALSE INFORMATION

Forged documents, impersonation, deliberate concealment, or material misrepresentation may result in rejection, removal of a Listing, suspension, account freezing, payment review, termination, and reporting to relevant providers or authorities.
