This Policy forms part of the RentDirect Terms of Service and applies to Tenant and Landlord subscriptions, rental payments, paid featured Listings, payment verification, receipts, refunds, and settlement workflows.

## 1. PAID SERVICES

RentDirect may offer:

* Tenant and Landlord subscription plans;

The relevant checkout or payment page controls the amount, currency, duration, payment method, and conditions for that transaction.

## 3. SUBSCRIPTION PERIOD AND RENEWAL

Monthly subscriptions are intended to last for the period displayed at purchase and yearly subscriptions for the displayed annual period. The current Platform does not automatically renew subscriptions unless a separate checkout notice expressly says otherwise.

Users must monitor expiry. A plan may become inactive when its payment is not completed, its term expires, a refund or reversal occurs, or the account is restricted under the Terms.

RentDirect may change prices or benefits for future purchases. A change does not retroactively alter a completed term except where law or the displayed transaction terms permit it.

## 4. RENTAL PAYMENT BREAKDOWN

Before a rental payment, the Platform may show:

* annual rent;
* refundable security deposit;
* administration or service fee;
* total Booking amount;
* amount already paid; and
* remaining balance.

The current calculation uses a refundable security deposit equal to 10% of annual rent and an administration fee equal to 10% of annual rent. The final amount shown at checkout, including any applicable taxes, provider charges, adjustments, or lawful changes, controls.

The security deposit is described as refundable subject to the tenancy agreement, applicable law, lawful deductions, and the actual condition and obligations of the tenancy. RentDirect does not decide disputed deductions unless a separate written service says it will do so.

## 5. PARTIAL AND REMAINING PAYMENTS

Where the Platform allows partial payment, each completed payment reduces the recorded balance. A Tenant must not pay more than the remaining balance through the Platform. A payment may remain pending or processing until provider verification is complete.

The Booking record and provider-confirmed status, not a redirect page or screenshot alone, determine whether the Platform treats a payment as completed.

## 6. PAYMENT PROVIDERS

Supported payments may include card and bank transfer through Flutterwave or another approved provider. Providers may impose their own terms, limits, identity checks, fees, cut-off times, bank charges, and processing delays.

RentDirect may receive provider status, transaction reference, payment channel, bank-transfer, virtual-account, webhook, and reconciliation information. Full card numbers, PINs, passwords, and OTPs must never be shared with RentDirect staff or another User.

## 7. PAYMENT VERIFICATION AND ERRORS

RentDirect may verify amount, currency, reference, customer information, provider status, and transaction integrity before recording a payment as completed. A mismatch, duplicate, suspicious transaction, chargeback, reversal, or provider error may cause a payment to be failed, held for review, or corrected.

Report an incorrect amount, duplicate charge, unrecognised transaction, or missing receipt promptly at **info@rentdirect.homes** with the payment reference and Booking information.

## 8. SETTLEMENTS AND LANDLORD PAYOUTS

Payment records may be allocated to settlement purposes including RentDirect operations, a tenant caution fee, and Landlord rent. A background process may release payout-ready settlements only after the required progress conditions, bank information, provider checks, and operational controls are satisfied.

Landlords must provide accurate payout information and remain responsible for tax, reporting, tenancy, and property obligations. RentDirect does not promise a payout date where a condition, bank, provider, compliance, dispute, or technical issue remains outstanding.

## 9. FEATURED LISTINGS

Landlords may request paid featured placement for an eligible Listing. The Platform will show the amount, selected duration, payment status, and expiry before payment. Featured placement improves visibility but does not guarantee enquiries, tenants, rankings outside the specified feature, or rental success.

RentDirect may remove or pause a featured Listing for fraud, inaccuracy, unavailability, policy breach, failed verification, or another lawful reason. The effect on a paid feature and any remedy will be assessed under the displayed terms and applicable law.

## 10. SUBSCRIPTION AND FEATURED-PAYMENT REFUNDS

Except where required by applicable law or expressly stated at checkout, completed subscription and featured-placement fees are not refundable merely because a User does not use the feature, does not find a property or Tenant, or changes their mind after the feature has been delivered.

Rental-payment cancellations, refunds, deposit treatment, and deductions depend on the Booking, tenancy agreement, provider status, circumstances of cancellation, and applicable law. RentDirect may investigate and communicate with the parties but is not automatically responsible for a Landlord’s or Tenant’s contractual refund obligation.

If RentDirect or a provider confirms an error, duplicate payment, failed service, or other basis for a refund, the refund will be handled through the available provider or account process. Provider and bank timing may apply.

## 11. TAXES AND CHARGES

Users are responsible for taxes, bank charges, provider charges, withholding, transfer costs, and other amounts imposed on them by law or by a displayed transaction term. RentDirect will show material charges before payment where required.

## 12. FRAUD, CHARGEBACKS, AND ACCOUNT RESTRICTIONS

RentDirect may pause payments, restrict an account, investigate a chargeback, or recover an amount where a payment is fraudulent, reversed, duplicated, unauthorised, or made in breach of the Terms. Users must cooperate with reasonable verification requests.

## 13. RECORDS AND RECEIPTS

Users should retain Platform receipts, provider confirmations, transaction references, payment dates, and signed tenancy documents. A Platform receipt confirms a recorded transaction state; it is not proof of title, possession, or completion of every tenancy obligation.

## 14. CHANGES

RentDirect may change plan prices, benefits, payment methods, providers, fee descriptions, settlement controls, or refund processes for future transactions. Material changes will be communicated as required by law.
