## 3. DEFINITIONS

### Personal Data

Information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

### Processing

Any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, use, review, disclosure, transmission, analysis, restriction, deletion, or destruction.

### Sensitive or High-Risk Data

Information that requires heightened protection in context, including government identifiers, financial information, identity documents, criminal declarations, precise location, and information about a guarantor or household member.

### Data Subject

The person to whom Personal Data relates.

### Data Processor

A service provider that processes Personal Data on RentDirect’s documented instructions, such as hosting, email, payment, support, or verification providers.

## 4. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 4.1 Account and Authentication Information

We collect name, email address, password hash, role, email-verification status, account dates, login/session information, one-time-password metadata, and account-security events.

### 4.2 Contact and Profile Information

We may collect mobile number, profile photograph, state of origin, residence information, preferences, and other profile details submitted through settings or profile pages.

### 4.3 Tenant Profile Information

Where a Tenant completes a profile, we may process:

* first, middle, and last names, date of birth, gender, nationality, state and LGA of origin;
* residence country, state, city, LGA, address, length of stay, and housing status;
* employment status and, where relevant, employer, role, dates, work address, HR contact, and employment documents;
* financial and affordability information, including income, expenses, savings, bank details, current rent, and rental plans;
* guarantor and current or previous Landlord information;
* rental history, household details, pets, smoking, work-from-home, and home-business information;
* social-profile links;
* declarations about convictions, evictions, rent arrears, tenancy litigation, and disputes; and
* identity, passport, utility, employment, bank, guarantor, and other supporting documents.

You should not provide information about another person unless you have a lawful basis and authority to do so, or the Platform expressly requests it for a legitimate verification purpose.

### 4.4 Landlord and Corporate Information

We may process individual identity and contact information, Landlord verification type, corporate or registered business information, CAC-related details, authorised representative information, bank and payout information, and identity or corporate documents.

### 4.5 Listing and Property Information

We process Listing title, description, address or area, city, state, postal code, coordinates, property type, room counts, annual rent, deposit, availability, amenities, usage rules, ownership information, images, property documents, verification statuses, featured status, and property-related communications.

### 4.6 Transactions and Payments

We process Booking, payment, subscription, featured-listing, receipt, settlement, transaction-reference, payment-status, currency, bank-transfer, payout, and provider-response information. Payment providers process card details and other payment credentials under their own terms. RentDirect does not intend to store full card numbers.

### 4.7 Communications and Support

We process enquiries, direct messages, chat messages, reviews, feedback, complaints, support issues, community-chat content, attachments, moderation records, and relevant account or Booking references.

### 4.8 Technical and Usage Information

We may collect IP address, browser and device type, operating system, identifiers, approximate location derived from technical data, timestamps, pages viewed, searches, filters, clicks, referring pages, error information, and security events.

Cookies and browser storage may be used as described in the Cookies Policy.

## 5. HOW WE COLLECT INFORMATION

We collect Personal Data from:

* registration, login, OTP, and settings forms;
* Tenant, Landlord, and property-verification submissions;
* uploaded photographs and documents;
* search, favourites, enquiries, messages, reviews, and support activity;
* Bookings, payments, subscriptions, and payout workflows;
* cookies, local storage, server logs, and similar technologies;
* service providers and verification partners;
* another User where a legitimate rental relationship requires it; and
* public or official records where lawful and appropriate.

## 6. WHY WE PROCESS PERSONAL DATA

We process Personal Data to:

* create, authenticate, secure, and administer accounts;
* provide search, listing, profile, favourites, messaging, Booking, payment, subscription, support, and community features;
* verify identity, corporate status, property ownership, property information, and physical-property status;
* create screening categories or summaries for legitimate rental decisions;
* facilitate communication between a Tenant and Landlord;
* calculate and record rent, deposits, fees, balances, payments, receipts, settlements, and refunds where applicable;
* detect fraud, impersonation, abuse, unsafe Listings, payment risk, and security incidents;
* provide customer service, complaints handling, and dispute investigation;
* maintain business, audit, tax, accounting, and legal records;
* improve performance, safety, relevance, and Platform design;
* send operational communications, payment notices, OTPs, verification notices, and support responses;
* send optional marketing where permitted and where you have not opted out; and
* comply with court orders, regulatory requests, and applicable law.

## 7. LAWFUL BASES

Depending on the context, we rely on one or more lawful bases recognised by applicable law, including:

* consent, including consent for optional processing or certain verification activities;
* contractual necessity to provide the Platform service you request;
* legal or regulatory obligation, including payment, accounting, fraud, and recordkeeping duties;
* legitimate interests in operating a safe and effective rental marketplace, provided those interests do not override your rights; and
* another lawful basis permitted by applicable Nigerian law.

Where processing is optional or high-risk, we will seek additional notice or consent where required. You may withdraw consent, but withdrawal does not invalidate processing already carried out lawfully and may prevent us from providing a requested feature.

## 8. IDENTITY, PROPERTY, AND TENANT SCREENING

Verification and screening may involve NIN, BVN, CAC, identity documents, property documents, supporting evidence, manual review, Dikript, Prembly, or another authorised provider. Verification data may be cached or retained to avoid unnecessary repeat checks, subject to lawful retention and security requirements.

Landlords may receive a limited Tenant profile or screening summary where the Tenant has a relevant enquiry, application, Booking, or other permitted relationship. RentDirect seeks to limit unnecessary exposure of bank details, identity numbers, document contents, and other high-risk data.

Verification indicators and screening summaries are not public certifications and are not guarantees of safety, solvency, ownership, suitability, or conduct.

## 9. SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

We may disclose Personal Data to:

### 9.1 Other Platform Users

To connect a Tenant and Landlord, show a Listing, display an authorised public profile, support an enquiry or Booking, or enable a User-requested feature. The information visible depends on the feature, role, verification status, subscription, and applicable privacy controls.

### 9.2 Service Providers

Hosting, cloud storage, databases, email, monitoring, security, customer support, analytics, payment, payout, identity-verification, corporate-verification, mapping, and other providers that process data for Platform operations.

### 9.3 Professional Advisers and Regulators

Lawyers, auditors, insurers, financial institutions, law-enforcement bodies, regulators, courts, and government agencies where disclosure is lawful, necessary, or required.

### 9.4 Business Transfers and Safety

A buyer, investor, successor, or adviser in a merger, financing, restructuring, or sale, subject to confidentiality and applicable law. We may also disclose information to prevent fraud, protect people, enforce rights, or respond to an emergency.

## 10. PAYMENT DATA

Payments are initiated through supported payment-provider workflows. The provider may collect payment-card, bank, account, device, and transaction data under its own privacy notice. RentDirect receives the information needed to identify the Booking, confirm status, issue records, process lawful settlement workflows, and investigate disputes.

Users must not send full card numbers, PINs, passwords, OTPs, or banking credentials through RentDirect messages or to another User.

## 11. DATA RETENTION

We retain Personal Data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including account operation, active or historical tenancy records, verification, payment and tax records, dispute handling, fraud prevention, security, backups, and legal obligations.

Retention depends on the type and sensitivity of information, whether a User maintains an account, whether a Booking or payment dispute remains open, whether law requires retention, and whether the data is needed to establish or defend a claim. We will define and publish specific retention schedules as part of the final compliance programme.

When data is no longer required, we will delete, anonymise, or securely dispose of it subject to lawful exceptions and backup-cycle limitations.

## 12. SECURITY

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards appropriate to the risk, including role-based access, authentication controls, encryption or secure transport where available, access logging, service-provider controls, document restrictions, fraud monitoring, and incident response.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. You must protect your credentials and notify us immediately of suspected compromise.

## 13. INTERNATIONAL AND CROSS-BORDER PROCESSING

RentDirect and its providers may process Personal Data in Nigeria or another country where hosting, email, payment, verification, analytics, or support services operate. Where cross-border processing occurs, we will apply safeguards and notices required by applicable law.

## 14. YOUR DATA-SUBJECT RIGHTS

Subject to applicable law and lawful exceptions, you may have rights to:

* request access to Personal Data we hold about you;
* request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
* request deletion or restriction of processing;
* object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing;
* withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis;
* request portability where the legal conditions apply;
* ask about automated processing or the logic of a materially significant decision; and
* complain to RentDirect and, where appropriate, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To exercise a right, email **<info@rentdirect.homes>** from the account email and describe the request. We may need to verify identity, protect other persons’ rights, and apply lawful exceptions. We aim to respond within the period required by applicable law.
