# RENTDIRECT COOKIES AND BROWSER STORAGE POLICY

**Effective Date:** ____________________  
**Last Updated:** ____________________

## 1. PURPOSE

This Cookies Policy explains how RentDirect uses cookies, local storage, session storage, pixels, logs, and similar browser technologies when you access the RentDirect website or web application (the “Platform”). It should be read with the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Cookies and browser storage help RentDirect keep users signed in, protect sessions, remember settings, operate the marketplace, understand errors, and improve the experience. Where a technology is not strictly necessary, RentDirect will provide the choice or notice required by applicable law.

## 2. WHAT ARE COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES?

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. Similar technologies include:

* **Session storage**, which usually lasts until a browser tab or session ends;
* **Local storage**, which can retain preferences or a limited account state on a device;
* **Pixels and tags**, which may help measure an email or page interaction;
* **Device and browser identifiers**, which help prevent abuse and secure sessions; and
* **Server logs**, which record requests, errors, security events, and technical information.

## 3. HOW RENTDIRECT USES THESE TECHNOLOGIES

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary Authentication and Security

RentDirect uses cookies or browser storage to support access tokens, refresh sessions, CSRF protection where applicable, role-based access, fraud prevention, rate limiting, and account security. Disabling these technologies may prevent login or protected features from working.

### 3.2 Platform Functionality

We may use browser storage to remember temporary selections, search state, preferences, form progress, feature access, or other information needed to provide a requested function.

### 3.3 Performance and Diagnostics

We may use logs and similar technologies to understand page loading, API failures, video or media delivery, browser compatibility, errors, and service performance.

### 3.4 Analytics and Product Improvement

If analytics tools are enabled, they may record aggregated or pseudonymous information about page visits, searches, clicks, feature use, and navigation. The final deployed Platform will identify analytics providers and provide any consent controls required by law.

### 3.5 Marketing

RentDirect will not use non-essential marketing cookies without the notice or consent required by applicable law. If enabled, marketing tools will be described in the relevant consent notice.

## 4. CATEGORIES

The Platform may use the following categories:

* **Necessary:** authentication, security, load balancing, session continuity, fraud prevention, and requested service delivery.
* **Functional:** preferences, saved state, interface choices, and support for optional features.
* **Performance:** diagnostics, error reporting, and service reliability.
* **Analytics:** aggregated measurement of Platform use where enabled and permitted.
* **Marketing:** advertising or campaign measurement where enabled and permitted.

## 5. INFORMATION THAT MAY BE ASSOCIATED

Depending on the technology, information may include IP address, browser, device type, operating system, session identifiers, timestamps, page or feature activity, referral source, approximate location, preferences, and security events. We treat information that can identify or reasonably relate to a person as Personal Data and handle it under the Privacy Policy.

RentDirect does not intend for cookies to store full payment-card numbers, passwords, NIN, BVN, or the contents of identity documents.

## 6. THIRD-PARTY TECHNOLOGIES

Third-party services may set or receive cookies or comparable data when used, including payment providers, identity-verification providers, cloud and security providers, mapping services, support tools, analytics providers, or embedded content. Their own notices apply to their processing.

RentDirect will maintain a current provider list or consent interface when the final production configuration is settled.

## 7. MANAGING COOKIES AND STORAGE

You can manage cookies through browser settings and, where provided, a RentDirect consent control. You may clear local or session storage through browser controls or by signing out and clearing site data.

Blocking necessary cookies or storage may:

* prevent login or session refresh;
* remove saved preferences or search state;
* interrupt verification, payments, or protected forms;
* make some pages or media unavailable; and
* require you to authenticate again.

Clearing storage does not automatically delete server-side account, Booking, payment, or legal records.

## 8. DO-NOT-TRACK AND DEVICE SIGNALS

Browser privacy signals may not be technically uniform. RentDirect will respond to a signal where required by applicable law and where the Platform supports the relevant interpretation.

## 9. RETENTION AND SECURITY

Necessary cookies normally last only as long as needed for a session or security function. Other technologies may persist for a period set by the relevant feature or provider. RentDirect applies the retention and security principles in the Privacy Policy.

## 10. CHANGES

We may update this Policy when our technology, providers, or legal obligations change. The latest version will state its effective date.

## 11. CONTACT

Questions about cookies or browser storage may be sent to **info@rentdirect.homes**. For broader data rights, use the process in the Privacy Policy.
