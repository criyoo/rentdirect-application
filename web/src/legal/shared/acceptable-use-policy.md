# RENTDIRECT ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

## USER CONDUCT, TRUST, SAFETY, AND SECURITY

**Effective Date:** ____________________  
**Last Updated:** ____________________

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) forms part of the RentDirect Terms of Service and applies to visitors, Tenants, Landlords, representatives, and all other Platform Users. It establishes minimum standards for lawful, honest, safe, respectful, and secure use of the rental marketplace.

## 1. GENERAL OBLIGATIONS

Users must:

* provide truthful and current information;
* use only accounts and documents they are authorised to use;
* comply with applicable Nigerian law and the law applicable to the property or transaction;
* treat other Users and RentDirect staff respectfully;
* protect personal information obtained through the Platform;
* use payments, messages, reviews, verification, and support tools for their intended purpose; and
* report suspected fraud, unsafe property conditions, impersonation, abuse, unlawful Listings, or account compromise.

## 2. FALSE INFORMATION AND IMPERSONATION

Users must not:

* create a fake or misleading profile;
* impersonate a Tenant, Landlord, owner, representative, RentDirect employee, payment provider, or government official;
* misrepresent identity, authority, age, income, employment, rental history, ownership, title, availability, price, property condition, or verification status;
* create multiple accounts to avoid restrictions or manipulate results; or
* use another person’s email, phone number, identity, document, photograph, or bank details without a lawful basis and permission.

## 3. FRAUDULENT OR UNLAWFUL DOCUMENTS

No User may submit or use forged, altered, stolen, expired where current information is required, or misleading:

* identity documents;
* NIN, BVN, CAC, or corporate records;
* proof of ownership or authority to let;
* property, utility, tax, employment, bank, guarantor, or rental-history documents;
* photographs or videos presented as the property; or
* payment confirmations, receipts, references, or verification results.

RentDirect may suspend access, preserve evidence, cancel a Listing or Booking, and report suspected fraud to relevant authorities or providers.

## 4. LISTING AND PROPERTY ABUSE

Landlords must not publish:

* a property they do not own or lawfully control;
* a duplicate, stale, unavailable, bait, or materially misleading Listing;
* a Listing with hidden fees, false amenities, fake images, false location, or deceptive availability;
* property that cannot lawfully be let for the advertised purpose;
* content that promotes unsafe, discriminatory, illegal, or exploitative housing; or
* a Listing designed to collect deposits or personal information without a genuine rental purpose.

Users must not reserve, report, review, or interact with Listings to manipulate ranking, availability, or trust indicators.

## 5. PAYMENTS AND FINANCIAL ABUSE

Users must not:

* request or send payment for an opportunity, verification badge, viewing, or tenancy outside the agreed payment workflow where doing so is intended to evade RentDirect controls;
* request passwords, OTPs, full card details, PINs, or banking credentials;
* submit a payment method they are not authorised to use;
* upload fake payment slips or falsely claim a payment is complete;
* reverse, dispute, or cancel a legitimate payment through deception;
* use the Platform for money laundering, fraud, bribery, illegal lending, Ponzi schemes, or other unlawful financial activity; or
* misrepresent the amount, purpose, recipient, or terms of a payment.

RentDirect may delay, reject, investigate, or reverse a workflow where a provider, bank, regulator, or security review identifies risk, subject to applicable law.

## 6. MESSAGING AND CONTACT INFORMATION

Messages and enquiries must relate to genuine property, tenancy, support, or Platform activity. Users must not use messages to:

* harass, threaten, stalk, intimidate, blackmail, or abuse another person;
* send spam, mass promotions, malicious links, malware, or phishing content;
* solicit unrelated products, political or religious campaigning, or illegal services;
* collect personal information for unrelated marketing, surveillance, or resale;
* bypass access controls or subscription limitations; or
* pressure another User to communicate or pay in an unsafe manner.

RentDirect may restrict the sharing of direct contact details or links in certain workflows to reduce fraud and preserve a transaction record.

## 7. HARASSMENT, DISCRIMINATION, AND EXPLOITATION

Users must not engage in harassment, hate speech, sexual exploitation, threats, violence, coercion, retaliation, defamation, discriminatory housing practices, or abusive treatment based on a protected or legally relevant characteristic.

Landlords must apply lawful, relevant, and consistently applied rental criteria. Tenants must not discriminate against Landlords, occupants, service providers, or other Users. Nothing in this AUP permits a selection criterion that violates applicable law.

## 8. PRIVACY AND DATA MISUSE

Users must not:

* scrape, harvest, download, or bulk-export profiles, Listings, messages, or databases;
* publish another person’s Personal Data without a lawful basis;
* use Tenant screening, financial, identity, guarantor, or Landlord information for unrelated marketing, employment, credit, political, or commercial purposes;
* sell, share, or reuse Platform data outside the legitimate rental purpose; or
* attempt to identify, track, or surveil another User without lawful authority.

The Privacy Policy, Verification Consent, and Landlord Tenant-Data Use Terms provide additional data rules.

## 9. REVIEW AND REPUTATION MANIPULATION

Users must not create or solicit fake reviews, review their own Listing through another account, threaten a User to obtain a review, exchange reviews for undisclosed benefits, or coordinate ratings to mislead the marketplace.

## 10. CYBERSECURITY AND PLATFORM INTEGRITY

Users must not:

* upload malware, ransomware, spyware, harmful scripts, or malicious files;
* probe, scan, attack, or overload the Platform or its providers;
* bypass authentication, rate limits, role restrictions, subscription gates, payment controls, or verification checks;
* access another User’s account, messages, documents, or data;
* reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract source code except where law expressly permits;
* use bots or automation to scrape, create accounts, send messages, manipulate Listings, or consume resources; or
* interfere with the availability, integrity, confidentiality, or performance of the Platform.

## 11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Users must have the rights needed to upload images, documents, logos, text, and other Content. Users must not infringe copyright, trademarks, trade secrets, privacy, publicity, or other rights.

## 12. ROLE-SPECIFIC RULES

### 12.1 Tenants

Tenants must not submit an application without genuine interest, misstate affordability or household information, use another person’s identity, damage a property during a viewing, occupy unlawfully, or make a payment promise they do not intend to honour.

### 12.2 Landlords

Landlords must not collect an undisclosed fee, misrepresent ownership or authority, advertise discriminatory terms, hide material defects, falsely label a property verified, disclose Tenant information, or approve a Booking while knowingly unable to provide possession under the agreed terms.

## 13. REPORTING

Report a violation through the Platform’s complaint or support flow or email **info@rentdirect.homes**. Include the Listing, account, message, Booking, payment, dates, and evidence where safe to do so. Do not expose identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information in a report.

RentDirect may ask for clarification, preserve evidence, contact affected Users, restrict content, or refer a matter to a payment provider, regulator, emergency service, or law-enforcement authority.

## 14. INVESTIGATION AND ENFORCEMENT

Depending on severity and risk, RentDirect may issue a warning, require correction or re-verification, remove Content, limit messaging or visibility, pause payments or Listings, freeze an account, cancel a feature, terminate access, or report conduct.

Enforcement may occur without advance notice where necessary to prevent harm, preserve evidence, protect the Platform, or comply with law. RentDirect may consider an appeal or review where the feature is available.

## 15. NO LIABILITY FOR USER CONDUCT

RentDirect does not control every User action and is not responsible for unlawful or harmful conduct by another User, subject to duties and liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded. Users should use safe viewing practices, verify identity and property information, and avoid sharing sensitive data unnecessarily.

## 16. POLICY CHANGES AND GOVERNING LAW

We may update this AUP as the Platform, threats, or law changes. The latest effective date appears above. Subject to mandatory law, this AUP is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and disputes may be brought before a court of competent jurisdiction in Nigeria.

## USER ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By using RentDirect, you agree to use the Platform honestly, lawfully, safely, and only for legitimate rental and support purposes. A breach may result in immediate account or transaction restrictions.
