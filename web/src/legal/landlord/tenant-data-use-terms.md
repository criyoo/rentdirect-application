# RENTDIRECT LANDLORD TENANT-DATA USE TERMS

## CONFIDENTIALITY, SCREENING, AND LAWFUL USE OF TENANT INFORMATION

**Effective Date:** ____________________  
**Last Updated:** ____________________

These Terms apply when a Landlord or authorised representative views, receives, downloads, discusses, or otherwise uses Tenant profile, screening, enquiry, message, Booking, identity, financial, guarantor, or rental-history information through RentDirect.

## 1. LIMITED PURPOSE

You may use Tenant information only to:

* respond to a genuine enquiry;
* evaluate or manage a rental application or Booking;
* arrange a lawful viewing;
* prepare or perform a tenancy agreement;
* manage property access, safety, payments, repairs, or rental progress; or
* address a genuine complaint, dispute, fraud, or legal matter.

You must not use Tenant information for unrelated marketing, employment, credit decisions, political activity, surveillance, public disclosure, resale, or creating an independent database.

## 2. MINIMISATION

Request and use only the information reasonably necessary for the rental decision or management purpose. Do not ask a Tenant to send a full NIN, BVN, bank password, OTP, PIN, full card number, or document that RentDirect has not required for a lawful purpose.

Do not copy, download, screenshot, or forward a profile or document merely for convenience. Keep records only where there is a legitimate, lawful reason.

## 3. CONFIDENTIALITY AND SECURITY

You must protect Tenant information from unauthorised access, loss, disclosure, or alteration. Access should be limited to authorised people who need it and understand their confidentiality obligations. Use secure devices and accounts, and do not send sensitive information over insecure channels.

Notify RentDirect promptly at **info@rentdirect.homes** if information is lost, misdirected, exposed, stolen, or accessed by an unauthorised person. You may also have separate legal notification obligations.

## 4. LAWFUL AND FAIR SCREENING

You remain responsible for the decision to accept or reject a Tenant. Screening information is an input, not a guarantee or instruction. Apply criteria that are relevant, lawful, transparent, and consistently used.

Do not discriminate, harass, retaliate, intimidate, or make a housing decision based on a protected characteristic or another unlawful factor. Do not treat an automated category, verification badge, public profile, or declaration as conclusive proof of character or affordability.

## 5. GUARANTOR, EMPLOYER, AND THIRD-PARTY INFORMATION

Information about guarantors, employers, household members, previous Landlords, or other persons may be subject to their privacy rights. Contact or verify such a person only for a legitimate purpose, with appropriate notice or authority, and without misrepresenting RentDirect’s role.

## 6. COMMUNICATIONS AND CONTACT

Use Platform messaging and approved contact channels respectfully. Do not pressure a Tenant to disclose personal information, pay an undisclosed charge, bypass a payment workflow, or move a conversation off-platform for the purpose of evading safety or recordkeeping controls.

## 7. RETENTION AND DELETION

When an enquiry, application, or tenancy relationship ends, stop using Tenant information and securely delete or return copies you do not need for a lawful record, dispute, tax, safety, or legal obligation. RentDirect may require deletion or restrict your access.

## 8. BREACH AND ENFORCEMENT

Misuse may result in removal of access, account suspension, Listing or Booking restrictions, mandatory re-verification, payment review, termination, provider notification, regulatory referral, or legal action. You are responsible for harm caused by your unlawful or negligent use.

## 9. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By accessing Tenant information through RentDirect, you confirm that you will use it only for a lawful rental purpose, keep it confidential, protect it appropriately, and follow the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, and applicable data-protection law.
