> **Important:** This is a working template for an authorised RentDirect agent, property professional, or lawyer. It is not a title certificate, valuation, survey, engineering report, or legal opinion. RentDirect must complete the contracting entity, fee terms, insurance requirements, professional-licence requirements, escalation contacts, and governing-law details with qualified Nigerian counsel before use.

This Agreement sets the minimum conduct, inspection, document-review, confidentiality, and reporting requirements for a person appointed by RentDirect (the **Inspector**) to carry out a physical inspection and/or document verification for a Landlord’s Listing.

## 1. APPOINTMENT AND SCOPE

RentDirect appoints the Inspector for the property, Landlord, documents, location, and scope identified in Schedule A. The Inspector accepts the appointment only to the extent that it is within the Inspector’s competence, professional authority, availability, and legal permission.

The Inspector must not expand the scope, promise a result, issue a legal conclusion, collect rent, accept a deposit, alter the Listing, negotiate a tenancy, or bind RentDirect or the Landlord unless a separate written authority expressly permits it.

The Inspector must promptly disclose any conflict of interest, prior involvement, family or financial relationship, referral fee, interest in the property, or other circumstance that could reasonably affect independence. RentDirect may reassign the appointment.

## 2. INSPECTOR QUALIFICATIONS AND REPRESENTATIONS

The Inspector represents that they:

* have accurately described their identity, business, employment, professional qualification, licence, and relevant experience to RentDirect;
* will carry out only work they are competent and authorised to perform;
* will follow this Agreement, the written scope, RentDirect instructions, and applicable law;
* will maintain any licence, insurance, permit, or professional standing required for the assigned work;
* will use reasonable care, skill, independence, and professional judgment; and
* will immediately report a limitation that prevents a reliable inspection or review.

A lawyer must state their jurisdiction of qualification and may provide a legal opinion or title-search conclusion only where separately instructed, competent, and legally permitted to do so. A non-lawyer Inspector must not present a comparison exercise as legal verification of title.

## 3. IDENTIFICATION AND CONTACT WITH THE LANDLORD

Before or at arrival, the Inspector should provide the appointment reference and show a RentDirect authorisation or other approved identification. The Inspector must use the contact details and communication channel approved for the appointment.

The Inspector must not:

* request or retain passwords, OTPs, PINs, full card details, or banking credentials;
* demand cash or an undisclosed fee;
* accept a gift, commission, referral benefit, or favour that could affect the result;
* imply that payment guarantees approval;
* threaten, harass, discriminate against, or pressure the Landlord or any occupant; or
* disclose personal contact information of a Landlord, occupant, Tenant, or other person without authorisation.

## 4. SAFETY AND ACCESS

The Inspector must assess whether the visit can be conducted safely. The Inspector may refuse entry, leave immediately, pause the visit, or request RentDirect support where there is violence, a threat, an uncontrolled animal, fire or electrical risk, structural instability, hazardous material, unlawful activity, crowding, severe weather, obstruction, or another material risk.

The Inspector must not force entry, climb unsafe structures, open locked areas without permission, handle dangerous materials, enter a private area against an occupant’s wishes, or conduct invasive testing. The Inspector should avoid visiting alone where the risk assessment or RentDirect policy requires a second person.

The Inspector must record a cancelled, incomplete, or restricted inspection accurately and must not convert an incomplete visit into a verified result.

## 5. PHYSICAL INSPECTION STANDARD

Unless Schedule A states a narrower or wider written scope, the Inspector should:

1. confirm the property address and the person providing access;
2. compare the visible property with the Listing and the inspection brief;
3. inspect reasonably accessible external and internal areas relevant to the Listing;
4. note material differences in rooms, fixtures, access, occupancy, location, condition, or stated amenities;
5. record visible hazards, restrictions, construction, access limitations, or conditions that could materially affect a rental decision;
6. avoid giving an expert conclusion outside the Inspector’s qualifications; and
7. explain any uninspected area, unavailable evidence, or limitation in the report.

The inspection is ordinarily visual and non-invasive. It does not establish structural integrity, electrical compliance, plumbing performance, environmental safety, building approval, planning compliance, valuation, measurement accuracy, boundary location, or habitability unless a suitably qualified person is separately instructed and the report expressly covers that subject.

## 6. DOCUMENT VERIFICATION STANDARD

The Inspector must identify each document reviewed and record, where relevant:

* document type, title, date, reference number, issuing body, named holder, and property description;
* whether an original, certified copy, ordinary copy, scan, or photograph was reviewed;
* visible alterations, missing pages, illegible details, inconsistent names, dates, addresses, signatures, stamps, or references;
* whether the document appears consistent with the Listing, physical property, Landlord, corporate entity, and stated authority; and
* any source, registry, professional, or legal check actually performed.

The Inspector must not state that a document is authentic, registered, current, free of encumbrances, or sufficient to establish title unless the Inspector has the appropriate competence, authority, evidence, and written scope to make that exact statement.

Potential forgery, competing ownership, mortgage, charge, lien, adverse claim, court matter, probate issue, boundary dispute, unregistered interest, or material inconsistency must be recorded and escalated. The Inspector must not conceal or resolve such an issue informally.

## 7. LAWYER-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS

Where the Inspector is a lawyer, the lawyer must state in Schedule A whether the appointment includes any of the following:

* document comparison only;
* a limited legal review;
* an official or professional search;
* a written legal opinion; or
* another defined legal service.

The lawyer must not imply that a physical visit alone constitutes a title search or legal certification. A legal opinion must identify its scope, material assumptions, documents relied on, searches performed, date, limitations, and the lawyer responsible for it. A lawyer must decline or escalate work that creates a conflict, exceeds competence, or would breach a professional duty.

## 8. PHOTOGRAPHS, COPIES, AND PERSONAL DATA

The Inspector may capture photographs, video, notes, location evidence, document images, and timestamps only when reasonably necessary for the assigned verification, safety, audit, dispute, fraud-prevention, or legal purpose. The Inspector must:

* obtain permission before photographing private areas or people, except where a lawful safety or evidence reason applies;
* minimise capture of faces, children, occupants, private belongings, identity numbers, bank details, and unrelated documents;
* use RentDirect’s approved device, account, storage, and transfer channel where provided;
* never store evidence in an unapproved personal cloud, public folder, messaging group, or removable device;
* redact or securely mark unnecessary personal data where practical; and
* report a lost device, misdirected message, unauthorised access, or suspected breach immediately.

The Inspector must not retain copies after the authorised retention period or use evidence for marketing, training, personal research, social media, unrelated clients, or any purpose outside the appointment.

## 9. EVIDENCE AND CHAIN OF CUSTODY

The Inspector must preserve the original state of evidence as far as practicable, avoid editing or enhancing images in a way that changes their meaning, and identify any crop, compression, transcription, translation, or redaction.

Each uploaded item should be traceable to the appointment reference, source, capture or receipt date, reviewer, and storage location. If evidence is received from a third party, the report should identify who supplied it and whether the Inspector verified that person’s authority to supply it.

The Inspector must not take originals away from the property unless the Landlord or lawful holder gives written permission and RentDirect’s process authorises it. Originals should be returned promptly and securely.

## 10. REPORTING

The Inspector must submit a complete report through the approved channel by the deadline in Schedule A. The report must distinguish clearly between:

* **observed:** what the Inspector personally saw, heard, measured, or received;
* **reported:** what the Landlord, occupant, official source, or other person stated;
* **checked:** what the Inspector compared or verified and how;
* **not checked:** what was outside scope or unavailable; and
* **opinion:** an assessment that is within the Inspector’s competence and expressly identified as an opinion.

The report should include the visit date and time, weather or access conditions where material, attendees, inspected areas, unavailable areas, documents reviewed, discrepancies, safety issues, photographs or evidence references, conflicts, limitations, recommended follow-up, and a proposed outcome of **verified**, **pending**, **requires clarification**, or **not verified**.

The Inspector must not select **verified** merely because a fee was paid, a Landlord was cooperative, a property exists, documents look plausible, or a Listing is commercially attractive.

## 11. ESCALATION AND INCIDENTS

The Inspector must immediately contact RentDirect at **info@rentdirect.homes** or the appointment contact for:

* suspected forgery, impersonation, bribery, coercion, fraud, or illegal activity;
* threats, injury, unsafe conditions, unlawful entry, or damage;
* a material conflict, adverse claim, court matter, charge, mortgage, tenancy, or boundary dispute;
* a request to falsify, omit, destroy, or alter evidence;
* an unexpected demand for payment or access to confidential information; or
* a suspected personal-data breach or loss of evidence.

The Inspector must preserve relevant evidence, avoid making admissions or promises, and cooperate with a reasonable investigation. Emergency services or an appropriate authority should be contacted where immediate safety requires it.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY AND RESTRICTED USE

All Listing, Landlord, occupant, document, report, and Platform information obtained through the appointment is confidential. The Inspector may disclose it only to RentDirect, authorised reviewers, the instructed professional team, a lawful regulator, law-enforcement body, court, insurer, or adviser with a legitimate need and lawful authority.

The Inspector must not publish, sell, scrape, reuse, reverse-engineer, or disclose the information, and must not contact a Landlord or occupant for unrelated business. These obligations continue after the appointment ends.

## 13. OWNERSHIP OF REPORTS AND RETENTION

Subject to the Inspector’s pre-existing materials, professional obligations, and applicable law, the Inspector grants RentDirect the right to use the appointment report and evidence for the Listing, verification status, Platform operations, safety, disputes, fraud prevention, audit, legal claims, and regulatory compliance.

RentDirect may retain records for the period reasonably required for those purposes. The Inspector must return or securely delete local copies when directed, except where retention is required by law or a professional duty; in that case, the Inspector must protect the retained copy and notify RentDirect where legally permitted.

## 14. INDEPENDENCE AND NO OUTCOME PROMISE

The Inspector’s compensation, appointment, or future work must not depend on a verified result. The Inspector must make findings honestly and must not change a result to satisfy RentDirect, the Landlord, a Tenant, or another interested person.

The Inspector must not promise that RentDirect will publish a Listing, display a badge, approve a Landlord, release a payment, or accept a Tenant. Only RentDirect’s authorised process determines the Platform status.

## 15. LIABILITY, INSURANCE, AND SUBCONTRACTING

The Inspector is responsible for negligent, reckless, fraudulent, unauthorised, or unlawful acts and omissions in carrying out the appointment, subject to applicable law and any separate written engagement. The Inspector must maintain any insurance or professional indemnity cover stated in Schedule A and provide evidence when requested.

The Inspector must not subcontract, delegate, or send a substitute without RentDirect’s prior written approval. An approved substitute must accept equivalent confidentiality, safety, qualification, and reporting obligations.

## 16. SUSPENSION AND TERMINATION

RentDirect may suspend or terminate an appointment immediately for unsafe conduct, conflict, dishonesty, confidentiality breach, unauthorised access, professional misconduct, missed deadlines, inadequate competence, or suspected fraud. The Inspector must stop using Platform information and return or secure evidence as directed.

Termination does not remove obligations concerning confidentiality, evidence, data protection, payment reconciliation, professional duties, or cooperation with an investigation.

## 17. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

This Agreement is intended to be governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, subject to the completed written engagement and any mandatory professional, consumer, employment, or data-protection requirements that apply. The parties should complete the dispute-resolution forum, notice address, and escalation process before execution.

## 18. ACCEPTANCE

The Inspector confirms that they have read and understood this Agreement, the written appointment, the inspection scope, the confidentiality requirements, the safety requirements, and the reporting standard. The Inspector accepts the appointment only if they can comply with them.
